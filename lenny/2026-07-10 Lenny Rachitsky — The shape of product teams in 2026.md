Kind: captions Language: en What does just the canonical product team look like in 2026? &gt;&gt; All the functions are starting to bleed into each other and the whole industry is wrestling with what that means. For the longest time at a big company like ours, the canonical team was something like two or three Android engineers, two or three iOS engineers, two or three server engineers, maybe a generalist, a PM, a designer, a data scientist if you're lucky. Maybe that's about it. But this year it's changing. We've adopted what we call pods, which are mini teams. Call it four to six engineers who are a bit more generalists. One we call product staff. Sort of an evolution of the PM. So a PM who can do some of what a designer does and some of what a data scientist does and some of what a research does. And then whatever specialist they need. Just by virtue of having less people to coordinate, they can often move faster and make better decisions.
