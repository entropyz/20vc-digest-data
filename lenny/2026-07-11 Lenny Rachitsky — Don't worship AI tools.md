Kind: captions Language: en The tools aren't all great. I think too often we have this really polarized binary outlook on the state of AI. Like are you AI pilled or are you anti-AI? People aren't binary and the state of the tools isn't binary either. They're amazing at some things and remarkably bad at others. The people who I think are going to make the most of it are the ones who are clear-eyed about what AI is good at and what it's not good at and also have an instinct or a nose for what it will be good at and not good at next month or in a couple months from now.
